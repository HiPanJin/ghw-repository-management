# Mergington High School Code of Conduct

This policy applies to this GitHub Skills training repository and its community.

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers welcome everyone to participate respectfully and without harassment, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our standards

We encourage welcoming language, respect for different viewpoints, constructive feedback, empathy, and a focus on helping the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language and imagery, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Responsibilities and scope

Maintainers explain and apply these standards fairly. They may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, comments, and public spaces where a person represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile for conduct concerns. Avoid posting private information in public issues. Reports should be handled confidentially and fairly; proportionate responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from the community.
